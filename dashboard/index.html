Methodology — every number, checkable

The question

What would this protocol’s token be worth if it launched one, with the protocol’s record exactly as it stands today? The radar answers with a pricing heuristic from the snapshot’s own figures, states the interval it deserves, and refuses to pretend the answer is precise.

The method (model radar-3)

r        = ln(mcap / TVL)            one per token-carrying protocol, ex-CEX
r_hat    = (n · median_cat + k · median_global) / (n + k),   k = 16
implied  = TVL · exp(r_hat)         50% interval from the category’s own p25–p75
  • Comparables: protocols with mcap_usd > 0 and category other than CEX — — of them in this snapshot.
  • Categories with fewer than 3 comparables price entirely off the global prior (median multiple —).
  • No growth or age multiplier, deliberately. On this snapshot, 7d TVL change correlates with ln(mcap/TVL) at ≈ −0.08 (n=119) and age at ≈ −0.04 (n=87) — indistinguishable from zero, so a multiplier would be noise dressed as signal. The dashboard shows these figures rather than hiding them.
  • The watchlist is the floor, never a ceiling. Early finds below the floor mirror the house rule as the ten fields allow: CEX and bridge/staking-product categories excluded, names matching wrapped/staked/staking or an exchange’s name excluded, version-marker names excluded.

What this does not know

  • No fees, revenue, emissions, unlocks or holder data — the snapshot carries ten fields and the model uses nothing else. Implied value here is TVL times a comps multiple, nothing more.
  • The DefiLlama record is not proof a token will or will not exist; a row may already have a token its record does not carry, and a watchlist row may never launch one.
  • The house rule that kept the watchlist leaned on a parent-protocol record this snapshot does not carry; the board’s early finds mirror its name and category tests, which is the closest the ten fields allow.
  • Nothing here is investment advice. Farm sizes are worked examples; airdrops are never guaranteed.
